## Visa Requirements for Visiting {{ site.country }}

---

Singapore's visa rules depend on your **nationality**. Many nationalities can enter visa-free for short visits, while others must obtain a visa **before** traveling.

- **No visa on arrival:** Singapore does not issue visas at the border. If your nationality requires one, you must have it before you fly.
- **Check your status:** Use the official ICA page to find out whether you need a visa: [ICA Visa Requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/visa_requirements).
- **Entry is never guaranteed:** Even with a valid visa or a visa-free passport, the final decision is made by the immigration officer at the checkpoint.

---

#### Requirements for All Travellers

- **Passport validity:** Your passport must be valid for at least **6 months** from your date of entry.
- **SG Arrival Card (SGAC):** Every visitor must submit this free online declaration **within 3 days before arrival** (including the day you land). This is not a visa. Submit it only through the [ICA website](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card) or the MyICA mobile app, and never pay a third party to do it for you.
- **Onward travel and funds:** You may be asked to show proof of onward or return travel and sufficient funds for your stay.
- **Airline checks:** Airlines may verify your documents, including the SG Arrival Card, before boarding. An incomplete submission can mean being denied boarding, so complete it before you go to the airport.
- **Immigration clearance:** Biometric verification (such as facial and fingerprint scans) is carried out on arrival.
- **Length of stay:** Visitors normally receive a short-term Visit Pass on arrival. The permitted stay depends on your passport, typically 30 or 90 days, and is shown in the e-mail or stamp you receive after entry.
- **Health:** There are currently no COVID-19 entry requirements. Check the ICA and Ministry of Health websites for any changes before travelling.

---

#### If You Need a Visa

1. **Confirm** on the ICA website that your nationality requires a visa.
2. **Apply early.** Visa applications are usually submitted through an ICA-authorised agent or a Singapore embassy or high commission, and processing can take several days or longer.
3. **Prepare your documents.** These typically include a valid passport, a passport-style photo, travel details, and proof of accommodation. Requirements vary, so check what applies to you.
4. **Request an invitation letter**. Email {{ site.author_email }} to request a Letter of Invitation after registration is complete.
5. **Wait for approval** before booking non-refundable flights.

Approval is at the sole discretion of ICA, and fees and processing times may vary.

---

#### Transit Through Singapore

- If you stay in the **transit area** of Changi Airport and do not clear immigration, you generally do not need an SG Arrival Card.
- If you leave the transit area (for example, for a long layover), you must meet the normal entry requirements, submit the SG Arrival Card, and clear immigration.
