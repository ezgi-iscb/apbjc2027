### {{ site.iteration }} Asia & Pacific Bioinformatics Joint Conference

We are delighted to invite you to the 2nd Asia & Pacific Bioinformatics Joint Conference (APBJC 2027), to be held at SUNTEC Singapore from September 28 to October 1, 2027.

Bioinformatics, the interdisciplinary field that unites the life and information sciences, is attracting unprecedented attention. Vast volumes of biological data, from genomic sequences to sensor readings, are now available, and computational science, including artificial intelligence, is advancing at a remarkable pace. Against this backdrop, bioinformatics has a growing role to play in helping us understand the mechanisms of disease, sustain healthy lives, improve agriculture, protect the global environment, and efficiently produce valuable substances.

The Asia-Pacific region has a strong need for the continued development of bioinformatics, and in 2027 Singapore will bring this ambition to life. The conference is hosted by the [International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB)](https://www.iscb.org/), the [Japanese Society for Bioinformatics (JSBi)](https://www.jsbi.org/), and the [Asia-Pacific Bioinformatics Network (APBioNET)](https://www.apbionet.org/), the leading organizations driving bioinformatics research and education in the region. Together, we aim to make this a memorable event that accelerates the advancement of bioinformatics across Asia and the Pacific.
