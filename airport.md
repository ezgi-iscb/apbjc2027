#### Things to Know at the Airport

- **Airport:** Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) has four terminals (T1-T4). Check which terminal your flight arrives at, as the taxi, bus, and rental counters are in each terminal's arrival hall.
- **Venue address:** Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre, 1 Raffles Boulevard, Suntec City, Singapore 039593.
- **Distance and time:** About 20 km, roughly 25-35 minutes by road depending on traffic.
- **Arrival card:** Most visitors must submit the SG Arrival Card online, free of charge, within 3 days before arriving. Check the [ICA website](https://www.ica.gov.sg) for current requirements.
- **Connectivity:** Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the airport. SIM cards and eSIMs can be bought at arrival halls.
- **Currency and power:** Singapore dollars (SGD). Power outlets are Type G (UK-style, 230 V).

---

#### Transportation Options

##### 🚌 By Bus

- **Where:** Public bus stops are at the basement of Terminal 1 and Terminal 3 and at the ground level of Terminal 2. Terminal 4 has its own bus stop.
- **Route:** Bus 36 runs from Changi Airport towards the city and stops near Suntec City. Use Google Maps or the SG BusLeh app to confirm the best route and stop on the day you travel.
- **Cost:** About S$2.50 or less with a contactless bank card or EZ-Link card. Have small change if paying cash, as no change is given.
- **Time:** About 60-75 minutes, depending on traffic and stops.
- **Tip:** Buses can be crowded with luggage. The MRT (see below) is usually faster and more predictable.

---

##### 🚇 By MRT (Railway)

- **Where:** Changi Airport MRT station is connected to Terminals 1, 2, and 3 (follow the "Train to City" signs). Terminal 4 is not directly on the MRT, so check airport signage for the shuttle or connecting options.
- **Cost:** Roughly S$2-2.50, payable with a contactless bank card, EZ-Link card, or SimplyGo. Fares vary slightly with distance.
- **Time:** About 40-50 minutes including transfers.
- **Hours:** Trains typically run from about 5:30 am to midnight. Check the [SMRT](https://www.smrt.com.sg) site for the latest schedule.

**Route Option 1: via the Downtown Line (one transfer)**
1. Board the train at **Changi Airport** (CG2) towards Tanah Merah.
2. Get off at **Expo** (CG1/DT35), one stop away.
3. Transfer to the **Downtown Line** (blue) towards Bukit Panjang / Downtown.
4. Get off at **Promenade** (DT15/CC4). Suntec City is connected by a covered walkway, about a 5-minute walk.

**Route Option 2: via the East-West Line (fewer stops, a short walk at the end)**
1. Board at **Changi Airport** towards Tanah Merah.
2. Change at **Tanah Merah** (EW4) to the East-West Line (green) towards Tuas Link.
3. Get off at **City Hall** (EW13/NS25). Suntec City is about a 10-minute walk through the underground link.

**Tip:** With luggage, use the Downtown Line route to Promenade, since it avoids the longer walk from City Hall. The station staff and the MRT app (or Google Maps) can confirm the fastest option on the day.

---

##### 🚕 By Taxi

- **Where:** Follow the "Taxi" signs in the arrival hall to the taxi queue. Ride-hailing apps such as Grab and Gojek also operate with designated pick-up points.
- **Cost:** Typically about S$25-35 on the meter, plus an airport surcharge (higher on Friday to Sunday evenings) and possible peak-hour or midnight surcharges.
- **Time:** About 20-30 minutes.
- **Tell the driver:** "Suntec City, 1 Raffles Boulevard." Taxis can drop off at the convention centre entrance.
- **Payment:** Most taxis accept cash and credit or debit cards.

---

##### 🚗 Car Rental

- **Where:** Rental counters are located in the arrival halls of the airport terminals. Booking online in advance is recommended.
- **Requirements:** A valid driving licence (an International Driving Permit may be needed if your licence is not in English) and a credit card.
- **Driving in Singapore:** Traffic drives on the **left**. Roads in the city centre may have ERP (electronic road pricing) tolls, which rental cars handle automatically and bill later.
- **Directions:** Take the ECP (East Coast Parkway) towards the city, then exit towards Suntec City or Raffles Boulevard.
- **Parking:** Suntec City has on-site car parks. Rates apply and may be higher during events.
- **Note:** For a short stay, a taxi or MRT is usually cheaper and less stressful than renting a car.
