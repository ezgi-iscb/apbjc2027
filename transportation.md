More than 140 stations across six MRT lines and 40 stations across two LRT lines span the island. The [Land Transport Authority website](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/rail_network.html) has more information about the rail network, as well as downloadable maps.

[You can additionally plan your journey by using LTA's journey planner or transport tools](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/plan_your_journey.html).


#### Paying for Your Ride

Use a stored value EZ-Link card/NETS Prepaid card/Concession card or SimplyGo EZ-Link/Concession card where fares are charged based on distance travelled. 

If you are paying adult fare, you may also use your American Express, Mastercard® or Visa contactless credit and debit cards, mobile wallets*, or NETS Tap contactless bank cards to pay for your train and bus fares, and skip top-ups to a separate transit card altogether.

Please note that single trip tickets have been phased out since March 2022.

\* ApplePay, Google Pay, SamsungPay, Fitbit Pay, Garmin Pay, Singtel Dash

## Bus Network

Weaving into the heartlands, business hubs and tourist attractions, buses bring you closer to your destination. They are one of the most convenient and accessible modes of public transport in Singapore.

Please visit the [Bus Router Singapore website](https://busrouter.sg/?lng=en#/) for detailed route and bus stop information.
