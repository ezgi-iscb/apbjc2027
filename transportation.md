## Rail Network

  <figcaption>Singapore Rail Map</figcaption>
</figure>

Today, more than 140 stations across six MRT lines span the island. This 200km system has over three million daily ridership. In addition, there are more than 40 stations across two LRT lines and this 28km system has over 200 thousand daily ridership.

The train system is currently run by two public transport operators – [SMRT Corporation Ltd (SMRT)](http://www.smrt.com.sg/) and [SBS Transit (SBST)](https://www.sbstransit.com.sg/).

* SMRT operates the North-South Line, East-West Line, Circle Line, Thomson-East Coast Line and the Bukit Panjang LRT. 
* SBST operates the North East Line and the Downtown Line, and the Sengkang-Punggol LRT.

[You can plan your journey by using LTA's journey planner or transport tools](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/plan_your_journey.html#paying_for_your_ride).


### Paying for Your Ride

Use a stored value EZ-Link card/NETS Prepaid card/Concession card or SimplyGo EZ-Link/Concession card where fares are charged based on distance travelled. 

If you are paying adult fare, you may also use your American Express, Mastercard® or Visa contactless credit and debit cards, mobile wallets*, or NETS Tap contactless bank cards to pay for your train and bus fares, and skip top-ups to a separate transit card altogether.

Please note that single trip tickets have been phased out since March 2022.

\*\ *ApplePay, Google Pay, SamsungPay, Fitbit Pay, Garmin Pay, Singtel Dash*
